What these bars mean

On this test, HDBSCAN finds more positives at small budgets; sampled K-Means finds more at larger budgets. The full-training K-Means bar uses more training data and a different score, so it is a separate reference.

04 / Model monitoring

The baseline deserves scrutiny, too.

Training-only feature bins are compared with future cases. A drift alert calls for investigation; it does not automatically retrain the model.

IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

About 22 out of every 100 earlier account-days had a two-way transfer on the same day. Later it was about 2 out of 100. This was measured on simulated data.

One unusual first day drives much of this difference. The alert tells us to check that data; it does not prove that the model has become less accurate.
